Game Information
Title: Super Off Road
URL: https://github.com/dopazz/SORP

Super Off Road was a 1989 arcade game developed by Leland.  It was ported to several home computer architectures by Graftgold in 1990.  SORP requires the original game data from the DOS version of Super Off Road.

Race with three players simultaneously and try to beat Ivan 'Ironman' Stewart in a nitro-boosted off-road stadium truck race!
Collect cash and nitro pickups as you drive!  Use the cash to buy truck performance upgrades or nitro.
If you are beaten by a computer-driven truck you lose a credit.
You can also convert your credits into cash and upgrade your truck early.

Enhancements:
Shaders can be applied at three levels: sprites, background, and whole screen.
Sound mode can be changed on the fly, with AdLib and PC speaker emulation.
Arcade Track-Pak levels can be played in SORP if you supply the original arcade chip files.
-> You can choose to race the original 8 courses, the 8 Track-Pak expansion courses, or a new blended championship of all 16 courses.

Instructions:
Add Original DOS Game Data
-> Copy the original DOS game's files into ports/superoffroad/gamedata/.
-> SORP checks every file and names any that are missing or wrong in ports/superoffroad/log.txt.

Optional: Enhance with Arcade ROMs
-> Copy the original arcade chip files into ports/superoffroad/gamedata/trackpak.
-> Chip files can be zipped or in a named subfolder (i.e. ports/superoffroad/gamedata/trackpak/offroadt/)
-> SORP will try to use "offroad" and "offroadt" for arcade assets:
-> -> Track artwork (downscaled to DOS resolution 320x200 but with greater color depth)
-> -> Music & Effects (Leland 80186 Audio System emulation)

| Button | Action |
|--|--|
|DPAD| Steer, accelerate (up) |
|Left Stick| Steer, accelerate (up) |
|A| Nitro; join the game |
|B| Nitro; join the game |
|Select| Options, with "Exit SORP" in the bottom left and "About" in the bottom right |
|Start| Confirm "Exit SORP?" |
|Hotkey + Start| Quit at once |
|Select + Start| Quit at once, for handhelds without a Hotkey button |

To accelerate with a button instead, set the options' "gamepad controls" to "B accel" (B accelerates, A fires nitro) or "A accel" (A accelerates, B fires nitro); up then does nothing in a race.
